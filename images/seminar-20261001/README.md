Create a polished editorial caricature illustration for a Korean engineering graduate-school seminar, very wide landscape 3:2 canvas, pure white background, restrained navy ink outlines with muted khaki, blue and warm coral watercolor accents. Two clearly separated equal horizontal rows of THREE small scenes, generous whitespace. No explosions, no violence. BOTTOM ROW: matching three small engineering office scenes, young adult office workers at desks: left CAD mechanical part and triangular mesh on two screens; center person clicking a simulation run play button, colored engineering contour plot on screen; right person organizing stacked files and searching articles on computer. All characters mildly exaggerated oversized heads, relatable and thoughtful, never mocking. Show simple routine and checklist repetition visually. NO text, NO letters, NO labels, NO title, no arrows, no watermarks. Crisp professional educational illustration, clear readable distinct vignettes.
